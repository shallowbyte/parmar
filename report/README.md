## On the colours

The categorical palette and its slot order are load-bearing rather than decorative: the order is what keeps adjacent series separable under colour-vision deficiency. Both palettes were checked with a validator, not eyeballed — 4 slots for line charts (worst adjacent CVD ΔE 9.1 light / 8.4 dark) and 3 for scatter, where every pair is effectively adjacent (worst all-pairs ΔE 9.2 light / 9.4 dark). Where more classes exist than the cap allows, the extra dimension moves to line style or grouping rather than to another hue. Corpus size and thread count are magnitudes, so where they are encoded by colour they use a single-hue ordinal ramp. See `../plots.py`.
